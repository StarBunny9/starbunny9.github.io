Puzzle Pals

Puzzle Pals is a game made to be displayed in the central library of Enschede and is made in collaboration with children from the International School Twente. They all made monster drawings and came up with various super powers for their monsters.

Puzzle Pals delivers an opportunity to explore the imagination of children and casually challenges the problem-solving skills of curious minds that are open to a short, positive, and brightening experience while exploring the library.
