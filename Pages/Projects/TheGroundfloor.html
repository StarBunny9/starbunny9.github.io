Project Description

The Ground Floor is a PS1 style movement shooter where the main character goes down through memory layers to get to the bottom of his subconsciousness.

Reflection

The Ground Floor is the very first game I created in a group project during my study at CMGT. Thus, it marked an important opportunity to discover my strengths and weaknesses within a team of six students. I already had experience from doing group projects during my Applied Physics study at the University of Twente. This made it easy to organize necessary meetings and communicate different ideas with each other. One difficult part of this was making sure everyone felt happy with the direction of the game. Everyone had a slightly different idea and standards, and sometimes this created some friction between the team members. This thought me how to give and receive feedback in a way where all sides feel heard and acknowledged while making sure the best solution is chosen. This also means that sometimes I had to set aside my own preferences for the benefit of the project.

Main Menu Loop

I also created the main menu loop for the game. All it took to make was some fog to hide where the elevator shaft ends, and a camera that moves down the elevator shaft and snaps back by exactly the length of a segment of the shaft. Then I gave the camera a spinning motion to give it an ever more slightly trippy effect.

Regardless of the scene's simplicity, the infinite, looping, spinning and descending visual is a nice way to reference to what the narrative of the game is supposed to be about, without giving away anything about the gameplay. And I am proud of the result!
