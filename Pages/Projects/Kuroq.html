Project Description

Kuroq is a local multiplayer game, where you fight each other over resources in a post apocalyptic setting. You can shoot, block or try to make your way to the loot, but you need be careful not to make your moves too obvious. You can play against friends or the computer in a bot campaign. The game is available for browser, Windows, and Android phones.

Camera System

I made a camera system that disallows children from freely moving the camera. Instead the camera turns to specific angles that are useful to the player in specific gameplay scenarios.

Monster Drawings

In this project we had to work together with 8-year-old children to figure out the best way to make the kids feel involved and their creative work and ideas to be incorporated. All while working towards a nice final product.

A lot of the work here included interpreting feedback about our concept art and prototypes for the monsters and the gameplay.
